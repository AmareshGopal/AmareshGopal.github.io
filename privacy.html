Pahunch privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Pahunch is an electric vehicle trip planner made by Amaresh Gopal, India. This policy explains what the app uses, why, and who it is shared with.

What the app uses

  • Your location, to start trips from where you are, follow your progress on a planned route, and find chargers near you. Location is used only while the app or its Android Auto screen is open.
  • Places you search for and your destination, to plan the route.
  • What you enter: your car, battery percentage, trip settings and the battery you arrived with. These stay on your phone.

Who it is shared with

To plan routes and find chargers, the app sends locations and search words directly from your phone to:

  • Google Maps Platform (maps, routes, places, elevation and weather). Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Open Charge Map (charger listings), when enabled. See openchargemap.org.

Photos (Google Gemini). Only when you snap your dashboard or a charging receipt, that photo is sent to Google's Gemini API to read the battery %, range, odometer or price, and is then deleted from the phone. Nothing else is sent to Gemini.

Voice and sharing. Directions and alerts are spoken by Google navigation and your phone's text-to-speech. Pahunch does not use the microphone and does not record audio. Trip messages are only sent when you tap Share, through the app you choose (for example WhatsApp).

Google navigation. During a trip, Google's Navigation SDK receives your location and route to guide you turn by turn, on the phone and on Android Auto. Google's terms, accepted once before the first trip, apply.

Sign in with Google and backup (optional). If you sign in with Google, Pahunch saves a backup of your trips, AEC history, settings and learned car data to a private app folder in your own Google Drive. Only Pahunch can read that folder; it is not visible among your Drive files, and the developer cannot access it. Your Google account email is shown in the app and kept only on your phone. You can sign out at any time, and remove the backup by removing Pahunch's access in your Google Account (Security → Third-party apps). Android's own device backup may also keep a copy of this data in your Google account.

Pahunch has no servers of its own. It has no accounts of its own (the optional Google sign-in is only for your private Drive backup), does not show ads, does not use analytics or tracking, and never sells data.

Opening other apps

When you tap navigate or a charging operator's app, that app opens and its own privacy policy applies.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your settings, trips and learned car data are stored on your phone and, only if you sign in with Google, in your private Google Drive backup. To delete them, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app, and remove Pahunch's access in your Google Account to delete the backup.

Children

Pahunch is meant for drivers and is not directed at children under 13.
